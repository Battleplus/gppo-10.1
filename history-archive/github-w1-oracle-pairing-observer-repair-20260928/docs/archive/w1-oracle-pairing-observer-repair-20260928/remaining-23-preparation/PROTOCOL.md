This is a mechanism ceiling. It does not show that public history can predict the winning action, does not measure a learned predictor, and does not authorize training.

## Joint analysis

Analysis runs only after the reused unit plus the new output form exactly 24 unique parent/repeat units. Duplicate or missing units stop analysis; missing branch labels are not filled with zero.

Primary oracle utility excludes NOOP and compares the best hindsight non-NOOP branch with the Hungarian branch from the same public state. Secondary analysis may include legal NOOP. Oracle gain is nonnegative by construction because the Hungarian action is in the candidate set; this is not repeat stability or learnability evidence.

The frozen gates remain: opportunities in at least 12 units and 4 parents; 8-parent macro mean gain at least 0.01; at least 4 parents with mean gain at least 0.01 and positive gain in at least 2 repeats; and at least one material unit where both first commands were accepted.

The analysis reports winners, ties, public-state hashes, acceptance mismatches, both-accepted gains, completion, confirmation, energy, and unknown labels. Actions from different public states are descriptive only. Hindsight winners may benefit from unpredictable random luck.

Failure closes only this first-window, Hungarian-continuation ceiling on these development parents. Pass permits preparing a separate predictor study; it does not prove predictor learnability or world-model benefit.
